## 4\. Card Types

PagoPay offers two types of Prepaid Mastercard:

**Plastic Card:** A standard physical prepaid Mastercard with an issuance fee of USD $19.99 and a monthly maintenance fee of USD $3.50 (first month free). Crypto-to-fiat conversion rate: 3%.

**Metal Card:** A premium metal prepaid Mastercard with an issuance fee of USD $500.00 (covers the first two years). Renewal fee: USD $120.00 per year thereafter. Crypto-to-fiat conversion rate: 2.5%.

Both card types share identical transaction limits and spending capabilities. The Metal Card includes additional premium features (Luxury Marketplace, Private Jet Access, Exclusive Member Events, and 24/7 Concierge Support) that are not available on the Plastic Card. The choice of card type does not affect your compliance obligations. There is no fee to load converted funds onto your Card.

**Maximum Cards Per Cardholder:** Each Cardholder may hold up to three (3) active PagoPay Prepaid Cards at a time, in any combination of Plastic and Metal Cards. Each Card is subject to its own issuance fee, monthly fee (if applicable), and independent balance. If you reach the maximum of three active Cards, you must close an existing Card before applying for a new one.

## 5\. Adding Funds to the Prepaid Card

The funds available on your Card are limited to the funds that have been loaded onto the Card through the PagoPay application. You may add funds to your Card by:

- Depositing supported cryptocurrency to your PagoPay Wallet and converting it to fiat currency, which is then loaded onto your Card
- Receiving peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers from other verified PagoPay users

Cryptocurrency processing is handled through ALT5 Sigma. A conversion fee applies when cryptocurrency is converted to fiat currency through ALT5 (3% for Plastic Card holders, 2.5% for Metal Card holders). There is no additional fee for loading the converted fiat funds onto your Card.

Conversion rates are determined at the time your conversion is processed, and the rate you receive may differ from any quoted or displayed rate due to market movements, spreads, liquidity, and processing time.

Once a conversion is completed and the resulting fiat funds are loaded to the Card, the conversion cannot be reversed. We may, however, correct pricing, processing, or posting errors (including by debiting or crediting your Wallet or Card) to the extent permitted by law.

Cryptocurrency deposits are subject to network confirmation and compliance review. We may delay, reject, return, or freeze a deposit where required for compliance, risk management, suspected fraud, or blockchain/network issues.

Conversion and related cryptocurrency services are provided through third-party providers. We do not control blockchain networks and are not responsible for delays, congestion, or third-party service outages.

We will endeavour to load funds to the Card within a reasonable time after conversion is completed. Loading may be delayed until we have received confirmed and cleared funds. You will not be able to load funds to the Card once it has expired.

We reserve the right to accept or reject any request to add funds to the Card at our sole discretion. We may ask you to provide information about the source of funds being loaded onto the Card. You agree to provide such information accurately when requested and warrant that funds loaded onto the Card shall not be derived from or connected with any unlawful activity.

**FINTRAC Reporting:** We may be required to submit reports to FINTRAC in connection with certain transactions or activities, including reports relating to large transactions and suspicious transactions, in accordance with applicable law. We may treat multiple transactions as related where applicable law requires aggregation. You agree to provide information reasonably requested to support our compliance obligations. We may also file Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) regardless of amount if we have reasonable grounds to suspect that a transaction is related to a money laundering or terrorist financing offence.

### Monthly Fee Collection Policy

The monthly maintenance fee is automatically deducted from your Card balance on the first (1st) day of each calendar month for that month. Your first month of Card usage is free; the first monthly fee will be charged on the 1st of the month following your Card activation.

If your Card balance has insufficient funds to cover the monthly maintenance fee at the time of the scheduled deduction:

- You will be notified immediately via email and in-app notification that the fee could not be collected
- PagoPay will attempt to collect the outstanding fee again five (5) days after the initial failed attempt
- If collection continues to fail, your Card will be paused (temporarily suspended), preventing new transactions until the outstanding fees are paid
- If the monthly maintenance fee remains unpaid for three (3) consecutive months, your Card will be permanently closed and deactivated

Upon permanent closure due to non-payment, any remaining balance on the Card (less outstanding fees owed) will be returned to your PagoPay Wallet. You may request a withdrawal of those funds or apply for a new Card, subject to payment of all outstanding fees and a new issuance fee.

You agree to cooperate with our investigation, including providing information, documents, and (if requested) a signed statement regarding the circumstances.

You are strongly advised to change your default PIN immediately upon receiving your Card. You must keep your Card, card number, CVV, and PIN confidential and take every reasonable precaution to keep them safe, including:
