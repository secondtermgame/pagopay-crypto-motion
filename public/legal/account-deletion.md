# Delete Your Account

If you no longer want to use PagoPay, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how.

## How to request account deletion

Choose either method below. Both reach the same team and follow the same process.

### Option 1 — From inside the app

1. Open the PagoPay app and sign in
2. Go to **Settings** → **Account**
3. Tap **Delete Account**
4. Confirm your request when prompted

### Option 2 — By email

Send a deletion request to the address below from the email associated with your PagoPay account. Include your full name and the phone number on file so we can verify your identity.

[Email support@mypagopay.com](mailto:support@mypagopay.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request)

## Before you delete

Please make sure of the following before submitting your request:

- Your card balance is at $0 — any remaining funds must be spent or withdrawn first
- There are no pending transactions on your account
- You have downloaded any statements or records you may need

## What gets deleted

When your deletion request is processed, we permanently remove:

- Your profile information (name, email, phone number, address)
- Your sign-in credentials and device information
- Your in-app preferences and settings
- Any uploaded photos or documents not subject to retention requirements

## What we’re required to keep

PagoPay is operated by a FINTRAC-registered Money Services Business in Canada. Canadian anti-money-laundering law (PCMLTFA) requires us to retain certain records for a minimum of **five (5) years** after your account is closed, including:

- Identity verification (KYC) records
- Transaction history and related compliance records
- Records required by tax, audit, or legal authorities

This data is kept securely, accessed only when legally required, and deleted automatically once the retention period ends.

## Timeline

Account closure and deletion of non-retained data is typically completed within **30 days** of receiving a verified request. We will email you to confirm once the process is complete.

## Questions?

If you have any questions about your data or the deletion process, contact us at [support@mypagopay.com](mailto:support@mypagopay.com).
